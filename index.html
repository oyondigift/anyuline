Industrialization

Introduction

Industrialization is the process by which a country changes from depending mainly on agriculture and traditional methods of production to using machines, factories, modern technology and industries to produce goods and services.

Characteristics of industrialization

  1. Development of factories and industries.
  2. Increased use of machines and technology.
  3. Growth of manufacturing activities.
  4. Increased production of goods.
  5. Development of transport and communication.

Importance of industrialization

  1. Creates employment opportunities.
  2. Increases national income.
  3. Improves people's living standards.
  4. Promotes economic growth.
  5. Reduces dependence on imported goods.

Challenges facing industrialization in Kenya

  1. Shortage of capital.
  2. Unreliable electricity supply.
  3. Poor transport infrastructure.
  4. Shortage of skilled workers.
  5. Competition from imported products.

Conclusion

In conclusion, industrialization plays an important role in the economic and social development of a country. It promotes employment creation, increases national income, improves living standards and encourages technological advancement. Although Kenya faces challenges such as inadequate capital, unreliable electricity and limited technology, these challenges can be overcome through government support, investment, improved infrastructure and proper planning. Therefore, promoting industrialization is essential for achieving sustainable economic growth and development in Kenya.
